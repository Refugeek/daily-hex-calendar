A MOMENT TO GET ORIENTED

Three spaces for today.

Fit all twelve tiles onto the board. Leave only the highlighted weekday, month and day uncovered.

  1. Drag a tile from the tray, or select it and tap a board cell.
  2. Rotate 60° with R, the rotate button, mouse wheel, right-click, or double-click / double-tap a tile.
  3. Move a placed tile by dragging it. Drop it in the tray or use “Return tile” to take it back.

Invalid drops return the tile to its previous position. Keyboard: select a tile with Enter, use the arrow keys over the board, R to rotate, Enter to place, and Escape to cancel.

Only your first, unaided completion of today’s puzzle is eligible for a score. Changing dates or starting again makes the current attempt practice. Use Pause to stop the timer and conceal the board and tiles. Select Resume when you are ready to continue. Time away from the tab counts unless you pause first.

Tuesday, February 1 is the sole date combination that cannot be solved under the no-flip rule and is flagged as unavailable for scoring.
